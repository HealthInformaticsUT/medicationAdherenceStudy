# Medication adherence study

This is a demo Shiny dashboard visualising results from a population-level medication adherence study conducted on the EST-Health-30 (EH30) database, which covers approximately 30% of the Estonian population (2012–2023).
The demo includes the 20 most frequently dispensed medication ingredients identified in the diagnostics phase of an international federated medication adherence study.
Adherence is calculated using the CMA (Continuous Multiple-interval Measures of medication Availability) framework defined by Vollmer et al. (2012). Three measures are used: CMA5, CMA6, and CMA7.

This dashboard presents four types of results:

* Average adherence 
* Proportion of adherent patients
* Continuous adherence
* Break analysis 

NB! Press "Update content" when first opening each results tab.
